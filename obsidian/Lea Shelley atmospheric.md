# Lea · Shelley · atmospheric

Response to the paper on weightless agents, atmosphere, and geometries of attention.

Related: [[Peterloo and The Mask of Anarchy]] · [[Malm — Fossil Capital and Manchester]] · [[Siqi Shelley]] · [[Sean Shelley reception history]] · [[Millicent Shelley Wordsworth]]

**Verdict.** Shortest and tightest of the five, and the one that touches my own project directly. She has assembled the poem's vaporous register without knowing what she was assembling. One correction gives her the physics; one line gives her the thesis; and the word *steam* opens onto something larger than the paper.

---

## 1. The correction that gives her her physics

Her atmosphere thesis is right about arrival and wrong about outcome. **The Shape is not weightless.** St. 28: "It grew—a Shape arrayed in mail / Brighter than the viper's scale." Armored. And what it does is heavy: Anarchy ends "dead earth upon the earth" (st. 33) and Hope walks "ankle-deep in blood" (st. 32).

Follow the sequence and it is a **condensation cycle**:

| | |
|---|---|
| st. 26 | vapour — "Small at first, and weak, and frail / Like the vapour of a vale" |
| st. 28 | hardens — "It grew—a Shape arrayed in mail" |
| st. 33 | kills — Anarchy "Lay dead earth upon the earth" |
| st. 32 | liquefies — Hope "ankle-deep in blood" |
| sts. 35–36 | absorbed — the Earth "had felt their blood upon her brow," "her face had been bedewed" |
| st. 36 | returns as voice — "To an accent unwithstood" |
| st. 38 | terminates — "Shake your chains to earth like dew" |

Dew is precisely what vapour does when it condenses onto a surface overnight. **The poem is a weather system.** That is the exact version of what she is calling atmosphere.

> [!important] "Bedewed" is the hinge word
> Blood as dew, sitting between the crimson dew of st. 29 and the falling dew of the refrain. Three appearances, one substance changing state. I have put this figure in three marking notes; **it belongs to hers.**

## 2. Send her to one line

St. 30:

> With step as soft as wind it passed
> O'er the heads of men—so fast
> That they knew the presence there,
> And looked,—but all was empty air.

The multitude **knows** and then sees nothing. Failure of vision as the condition of political knowledge. That is the altered geometry of attention, in one line, and it is stronger than the paragraph she built around it.

## 3. The steam — why this paper matters beyond the seminar

Her catalogue is dew, mist, steam, light, vapour, clouds, wind. **Six are the register of what Malm calls flow. One is industrial.** And *steam up* is the verb the poem gives to the massacre itself:

> 'And that slaughter to the Nation
> Shall steam up like inspiration,
> Eloquent, oracular;
> A volcano heard afar.

In a poem about Manchester in 1819.

So Shelley's figure for revolutionary agency is the physical substance the mills were beginning to run on. **The historical qualifier makes this stronger rather than weaker:** in 1819 most Manchester mills were still water-powered (Chapman, via Malm), so steam was itself "small at first, and weak, and frail," a thing that grew. The same figure carries revolutionary emergence and the emergence of the power that would organise the world against it.

> [!warning] Status
> An available reading, not a demonstrated one. "Steam up" is ordinary usage for vapour rising. But the coincidence of vocabulary is exact, and she has arrived at it independently. See [[Malm — Fossil Capital and Manchester]].

## 4. Press her central question rather than accepting it

She asks: "Does Shelley locate the actual political event first and foremost here, in such an altered relation of attention?"

The poem also has sts. 83–88, which are strategic and cold: liability, then

> 'Every woman in the land
> Will point at them as they stand—
> They will hardly dare to greet
> Their acquaintance in the street.

then the regulars defecting from the amateurs (st. 88).

**Notice that this is also attention, and it is the poem's operative mechanism.** But it is attention as communal surveillance and shame, not as aesthetic reorientation. The poem holds **two theories of attention**, one Retallackian and one closer to the panoptic. Her thesis survives only if she distinguishes them, and that distinction is the paper. Cross-reference [[Siqi Shelley]] §3, where the same three stanzas do the work of correcting a different claim.

## 5. The reflexive doubling — keep the problem, do not solve it

Her claim that the poem's means of moving through the world are as weightless as its agents is contradicted by the poem's actual history: suppressed thirteen years, then travelling by mass-circulation print and recitation. **Vapour is a bad model for how it moved.** So the reflexivity is aspirational rather than descriptive, and the gap between the poem's self-image and its transmission is where this paper joins [[Sean Shelley reception history|Sean's]].

## 6. Two small fixes

- She has "Like the vapour **on** a vale." The text reads "of a vale" (st. 26).
- Cite Retallack: *The Poethical Wager* (University of California Press, 2003), where "Geometries of Attention" is a section title and her own formulation is "radically altering geometries of attention." Her spelling of the name is correct.
